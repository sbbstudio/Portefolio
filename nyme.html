It finds the job, writes the application, builds the site.

NYME finds promising roles early and starts working the moment one appears. One thorough application while the role is fresh, not fifty generic ones a week later.

Each run starts with the company: what it ships, what the role is really for and where the candidate may genuinely fit. Those conclusions remain hypotheses, not facts. Nine stages turn the work into a CV, cover letter and dedicated website. Nothing mass-sent. Nothing invented. The final decision stays with the candidate.
